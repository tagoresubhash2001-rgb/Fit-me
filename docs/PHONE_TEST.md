# Phone test checklist (Android)

Install FitMe from the email link sent by Firebase App Distribution. Open it and sign in with your Google account.
If something does not look right at any step, go to **Settings → Send diagnostics** and tap **Send diagnostics**.
That sends us a report of what the phone allows and what is connected. It never includes your health numbers.

| # | What to do | What you should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the app and finish the first questions (or sign in if you already did). | The Home screen with your name, calories, water and steps. A green card says everything is free during launch. |
| 2 | Go to Settings → Connected devices → **Count steps on this phone**. | A FitMe screen explains why it needs "physical activity". Tap Continue, then **Allow** on the phone's question. The row then says "Connected". |
| 3 | Walk about 100 steps with the app open on Home. | The steps number on Home goes up within a few seconds. Under it you see "From this phone". |
| 4 | Close the app completely (swipe it away). Walk another 100 steps. Open the app again. | The steps include the walk you did while the app was closed. |
| 5 | Restart the phone. Walk 100 steps. Open FitMe. | Steps keep counting from before the restart. They do not start from zero and are not counted twice. |
| 6 | Settings → Connected devices → **Connect Health Connect**. Allow the items on the list. | A list of the data FitMe can read with ticks. Steps, sleep and workouts from your watch or apps appear after a moment. Steps on Home now say "From Health Connect" and the app name (for example Samsung Health or Pixel Watch). |
| 7 | Connect your watch to Health Connect through the watch's own app (Samsung Health, Wear OS, Fitbit and so on), then tap **Sync now** in FitMe. | Your watch steps, heart rate and sleep appear in FitMe. Steps are never added twice. |
| 8 | Home → **Steps not updating? → Check**. | Four checks with green ticks, or a plain-language problem with a button that fixes it (for example "Open battery settings"). |
| 9 | Food → search **dosa**, then **idly**. | Plain dosa, rava dosa and more for "dosa". "Idli" shows even though you typed "idly". |
| 10 | Food → **Scan food** → Take photo of a meal. | A camera explanation first, then the camera. After the photo you see the foods it found with weights you can change before adding. |
| 11 | Workouts → **Track a route** → Walk → **Start**. Allow location "While using the app". Walk for 2 minutes with the screen off. | A map draws your path. Time, distance and pace go up. A notification says FitMe is tracking your route. |
| 12 | Tap **Finish and save**. | The walk is saved in your workout history with its distance. |
| 13 | Water, weight and sleep: add one of each. | Each appears on Home at once. Swipe an entry to delete it, then tap Undo. |
| 14 | Settings → Permissions. | Each permission shows its state (Allowed, Not asked, Turned off). Turned-off items offer "Open settings". |
| 15 | Open the bell at the top (after we send you a test message). | Our test message is in your messages. |
| 16 | Settings → Help and contact → send us a short message. | "Message sent". Our reply arrives in the bell messages. |
| 17 | Plans. | Your country's prices in your currency (₹ in India). During launch you are not charged anything. |

## iPhone (when the iOS app is available)

Do the same steps. On iPhone, step 2 asks for "Motion & Fitness" and step 6 uses Apple Health (Apple Watch data
comes through Apple Health).
