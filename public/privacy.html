FitMe

Privacy and health data

This page explains what FitMe does with the data you choose to give it. It is a summary for the app and health platforms; the full privacy policy and terms are published by Sindulis Innovative Solutions.

Health data from Apple Health and Health Connect

Only after you connect it in Settings, Connected devices, FitMe reads: steps, distance, active energy, heart rate, sleep, workouts and weight. FitMe never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.

  • It is used only to show your activity, sleep and progress in FitMe, and to give you suggestions inside the app.
  • Daily summaries are stored in your private FitMe account so you see them on every device. Nobody else can read them.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with third parties.
  • You can disconnect at any time, change permissions in Apple Health or Health Connect, and delete synced entries or your whole account in Settings.

Meal photos

When you scan a meal, the photo is sent securely to our server and to the AI service that estimates the foods, then discarded. It is not stored. You check and correct the result before anything is saved.

Profile and testimonial photos

A profile photo is stored in your account and shown where your name appears. Testimonial photos are seen only by our review team until you consent and the testimonial is approved; you can withdraw consent at any time.

Your rights

You can download your data and delete your account from Settings. Deleting the account removes your logs, photos and settings.
